# Retrospective - Iteration 1

## Did Well

- **Solid Technical Foundation**: Successfully built a functional baseline for the project, laying a reliable foundation for upcoming features.

- **Framework Validation**: Gained a clear understanding of our tech stack and verified that the chosen frameworks are well-suited for our project goals.

## Do Differently

- **Work Division & Organization**: Structure task delegation more clearly upfront to ensure work is evenly distributed.

- **Effort Estimation**: Improve capacity planning and individual effort estimation during sprint planning to prevent workload imbalances.

- **Team Communication**: Increase real-time updates across team members to catch dependencies and blockers earlier.

- **Board Hygiene**: Keep the task board continuously updated and disciplined so it accurately reflects live work status.

## Puzzles

- **Shopify Metafield Optimization**: We are still determining the best architecture for Shopify metafields. Currently, we are duplicating fields across different categories when a single, well-structured metafield could streamline the setup.
